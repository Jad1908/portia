Keep one thing this chat learned where the next session finds it: on the cards of the tables it is about.

`question`, `answer` and `so` are yours, in plain language; `so` is what it changed. `about` names the tables and columns it concerns (`table.column` or a table), both sides for a relationship; the finding is read from `describe_source` on those tables and nowhere else. `from` lists the `query_data` numbers from `review_queries` it rests on; their SQL and rows are copied in beside your sentences, so write the answer as a sentence rather than the numbers restated. `spec_name` names the model it was in service of, when there is one.

A join in a cited query between two declared tables is kept in the graph with the question as its reason; `joins` says what was kept.
