Ask the user one to four questions and wait for the answers. It is the only way a question reaches them: a question typed into your reply stops nobody.

Use it when you cannot go on without the answer and the checks cannot settle it. Each question has a short `header` (12 characters or fewer), the `question` in one sentence, and two to four `options` with a `label` and a one-line `description`; the user can also type their own. `multiSelect` when more than one can be true. The answer comes back as `{question: label or text}`.
