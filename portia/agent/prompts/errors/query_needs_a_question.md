Every `query_data` call needs a `question` — one sentence, in plain language, saying
what you are trying to find out. It is not paperwork.

At the end of a chat you will be shown every query you ran, so you can decide which
ones were worth keeping as findings. Thirty bare SQL strings are not reviewable, and
by then you will not remember why you wrote the seventh one. The sentence is what
makes that list readable, and it is what a person reads later when the finding turns
up beside a table they are working on.

Write what you want to know, not what you expect the answer to be:
*"are there any rank-100 events in Paris in summer 2025?"*, not *"probably zero"*.

Call it again with a question.
