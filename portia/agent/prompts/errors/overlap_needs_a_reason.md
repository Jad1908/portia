Every pair needs a `reason` — one sentence saying why you think these two columns
might be related. It is not paperwork, and it is not optional.

A measured overlap of zero means *these two columns share no values*. It does not
mean they are unrelated: `country_name` holding `France` and `country_code`
holding `FRA` overlap by exactly zero and are the same thing after a mapping.
Stored on its own, that zero reads as a dead end to whoever finds it next.
Stored with *"both look like they identify countries, one by name and one by
code"*, it reads as a work item.

So say what made you pick the pair — what you noticed in the summaries, the
column roles, or the project context. Not what you expect the number to be.

Call it again with a reason on each pair.
