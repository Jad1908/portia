Every `plot_data` call needs a `tab` — the short name the chart's tab carries on
screen. Two or three words that say what the picture is *about*, the way you would
title it: `Events per month`, `Hotels by city`, `Rank against price`.

**The name is the tab's identity, not just its label.** Drawing again under a name
already on the strip replaces that chart in place, keeping its position. That is how
you correct one: same name, better query. A new name opens a new tab.

So pick a name you would be willing to reuse. `chart 1` is a name nobody can reuse
on purpose, and a strip of `chart 1`, `chart 2`, `chart 3` is a strip you cannot read.

Call it again with a tab name.
